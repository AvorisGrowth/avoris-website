Website data notice

How enquiry details are handled.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Information collected

The Growth Check form asks for your name, business name, email address, selected priority, and any optional context you choose to share.

How it is used

Avoris uses these details to review your request, contact you about it, and understand whether its services are a suitable fit. Your details are not submitted to a mailing list through this form.

Form provider

The form is processed by FormSubmit and delivered to the Avoris business email. FormSubmit states that form submissions are retained for 30 days. Its own terms and privacy practices also apply when it processes a submission.

What not to send

Do not include passwords, payment details, government identification, health information, confidential customer data, or other sensitive information in the enquiry form.

Questions or requests

For a question about information submitted through this website, email avorisgrowthsystems@gmail.com.

This page is a practical website data notice, not a substitute for legal advice. Avoris should obtain qualified legal review before launch if its activities, customers, or hosting arrangements require a fuller privacy policy.
